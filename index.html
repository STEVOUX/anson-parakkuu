Play Anson Fly Online

To play this tap to fly game, simply click or tap the screen to make Anson jump. You can also use the Spacebar on your keyboard, or the 'A' button and Right Trigger (RT) on an Xbox Controller. It's an easy to learn, yet hard to master mobile web game that will test your reflexes!

Funny Flappy Bird Style

Unlike a traditional flappy bird clone, this funny game features a dynamic comedy system! When you crash into the Bharatgas cylinder towers, the game will roast you with hilarious excuses based on exactly how you failed. Did you panic tap? Did you miss by a single pixel? The game knows!

Features

  • Responsive 9:16 gameplay container optimized for mobile and desktop.
  • Dynamic day and night cycle that shifts as your score increases.
  • Context-aware blame system that makes fun of your crashes!
  • Full native Gamepad support with analog flap physics and dual-rumble vibrations.

Why This Game is Funny

Every time you play game online with Anson, you're guaranteed a laugh. The physics are perfectly tuned to feel like a meme flying game, with a massive "yeet" effect when you hit a pipe, sending Anson spinning wildly out of control while floating text displays his comedic last words.
