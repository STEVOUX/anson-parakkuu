Anson Parakkuu is a funny flappy bird style game where you control Anson Fly as he tries to fly through tricky obstacles. Whether you're playing on a mobile phone or a desktop browser, you'll experience a hilarious, fast-paced free online funny game full of funny crashes and relatable moments!

Play Anson Fly Online

To play this tap to fly game, simply click or tap the screen to make Anson jump. You can also use the Spacebar on your keyboard, or the 'A' button and Right Trigger (RT) on an Xbox Controller. It's an easy to learn, yet hard to master mobile web game that will test your reflexes!

Funny Flappy Bird Style Game
